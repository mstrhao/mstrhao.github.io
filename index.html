Research interests

My interests lie at the intersection of conscious access, working memory, and the geometry of neural population activity.

Conscious access
How does sensory information become available for conscious report?
Working memory
How are individual items maintained and organized into sequences?
Population representations
How does neural population structure support the transformation from sensory input to memory?
